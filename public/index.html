Welcome to EAM-WIFI

A short guide before you begin. Read it now or skip and return to it whenever you need help.

Important · Muhimu

EAM-WIFI manages Wi-Fi businesses, packages, vouchers and router integrations. Registering a router in the system does not connect a physical router automatically. Real internet access must be configured and tested on the actual router.

01
Create your account · Fungua akaunti

Register with your email, password, owner name and business details. Keep your login details private.

02
Register your Wi-Fi business · Sajili biashara

Add your hotspot site, location and router reference details. Do not enter router passwords or API secrets into ordinary registration fields.

03
Wait for platform approval · Subiri idhini

Your business remains restricted until the platform administrator confirms the required subscription. The current reference price is TSh 6,000 per month.

04
Set up packages and vouchers · Tengeneza vifurushi na vocha

After approval, configure packages and issue vouchers only after confirming customer payment. Test with sample customers before serving real customers.

05
Connect your router · Unganisha router

For MikroTik, prepare RouterOS, Hotspot, a secure management method, firewall restrictions and the correct integration settings. Follow the router guide in the Help Centre. Never expose router management ports to the public internet.

06
Test safely · Fanya majaribio

Use the built-in router simulation to practise the workflow. Simulation is not proof that a real MikroTik router, captive portal or internet session works.

You can reopen this guide later from “Help / Msaada” at the top of the page.

BUSINESS SETUP

Register your Wi-Fi business

Complete your hotspot site and router setup. After submission, call the Super Admin for account approval. Your service stays locked until approval.

Selected reference equipment

Default design: MikroTik hAP ax² as the Hotspot controller plus TP-Link EAP650-Outdoor in AP/bridge mode for the target 50–100 m outdoor coverage. The outdoor AP is separate from the router and is not yet paired or physically configured. See the hardware deployment plan.

Security notice

Do not enter your router password or API secret here. The address and port are saved only as setup references. They do not create a live connection by themselves.
