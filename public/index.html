Welcome to EAM-WIFI · Karibu EAM-WIFI

Quick instructions for the main features. / Maelekezo mafupi ya vipengele muhimu.

1. Account and business · Akaunti na biashara

Create an account, then register your business, hotspot site, business contact phone and customer payment phone. Add clear payment instructions. Keep your password private. / Fungua akaunti, sajili biashara, eneo la hotspot na taarifa za router. Usimpe mtu nenosiri lako.

2. Subscription approval · Idhini ya usajili

New businesses stay locked until the Super Admin approves payment. Reference price: TSh 6,000 per month. / Biashara mpya hubaki imefungwa hadi Super Admin athibitishe malipo. Bei ya rejea: TSh 6,000 kwa mwezi.

3. Packages and vouchers · Vifurushi na vocha

Each business owner creates and manages their own packages: name, price, connected-time or wall-clock duration, validity, data/speed limits and device limit. Edit, pause or reactivate packages; pause packages with voucher/sales history instead of deleting them. Confirm full payment before issuing a voucher. / Kila mmiliki anatengeneza na kusimamia vifurushi vya biashara yake: jina, bei, muda, kiwango cha data/kasi na vifaa. Unaweza kuhariri, kusitisha, kuwasha tena au kufuta kifurushi kisichotumika. Kifurushi chenye historia ya vocha au mauzo kisitishwe badala ya kufutwa. Hakikisha malipo yote kabla ya kutoa vocha.

4. Customer Wi-Fi portal · Tovuti ya mteja

Use “Open customer Wi-Fi portal” to view your public page. Customers see your payment number and package limits, pay your business directly, then receive a voucher from you after you verify payment. When they enter it from the router login screen, the portal signs them into the hotspot automatically. Mobile-money payment confirmation is still manual. / Tumia “Open customer Wi-Fi portal” kuona ukurasa wa wateja. Router lazima isanidiwe vizuri ili intaneti ifanye kazi.

5. Router tools · Zana za router

For MikroTik RouterOS 7.13+, select “Connect MikroTik router”, download the personalized installer, upload it in Winbox/WebFig Files and import it. Also apply the captive-portal redirect and walled-garden instructions shown by the dashboard. The router needs a working ISP/Hotspot setup and outbound HTTPS. Other router brands are not integrated yet. Physical router testing is required before production. Never expose router management ports or share router credentials. / Kusajili router hakuiunganishi moja kwa moja. Simulator ni majaribio tu. Uunganishaji salama umeanza, lakini kuwasha vocha moja kwa moja na udhibiti kamili wa router bado havijakamilika. Usifungue milango ya router kwa umma wala kushiriki nywila.

6. Messages and support · Mawasiliano na msaada

Use Messages for private or group chats. Super Admin can send platform broadcasts. Use the support form below to ask for help and check replies in “My support conversations”. / Tumia Messages kwa mazungumzo binafsi au ya kikundi. Super Admin anaweza kutuma tangazo. Tuma swali kupitia fomu ya msaada hapa chini na angalia majibu kwenye “My support conversations”.

7. Super Admin · Msimamizi Mkuu

The dashboard shows business, router, customer, voucher and subscription figures, plus system-health checks for stale routers, failed router events, sessions and support messages. Approve subscriptions after confirming payment, manage support requests, and open your own business separately. / Dashboard huonyesha takwimu za biashara, router, wateja, vocha na usajili. Idhinisha baada ya kuthibitisha malipo na jibu maombi ya msaada.

BUSINESS SETUP

Register your Wi-Fi business

Complete your hotspot site and router setup. After submission, call the Super Admin for account approval. Your service stays locked until approval.

MikroTik Hotspot is the only automated router integration currently available. Other brands can be recorded, but cannot activate customer vouchers until their connector is built and tested.

Selected reference equipment

Default design: MikroTik hAP ax² as the Hotspot controller plus TP-Link EAP650-Outdoor in AP/bridge mode for the target 50–100 m outdoor coverage. The outdoor AP is separate from the router and is not yet paired or physically configured. See the hardware deployment plan.

Security notice

Do not enter your router password or API secret here. The address and port are saved only as setup references. They do not create a live connection by themselves.
